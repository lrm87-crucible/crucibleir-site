Request a pilot

Tell us about your idle assets.

We’re starting with a small group of founding pilot partners. Share a few details and we’ll reach out to set up a short call.

What founding pilot partners get

  • We load your asset list for you. Send a spreadsheet in whatever shape it’s in. Your team doesn’t spend its time on setup.
  • A written baseline report, yours to keep. What you hold, what it’s worth, and what it costs you to hold it — whether or not you continue with us afterwards.
  • Founding pricing, locked. If you continue, you keep the rate we agree now.
  • Direct influence on what gets built. Early partners shape the roadmap rather than waiting behind a thousand other customers.
  • Your data out at any time. Full export in a common format, on request, at no charge. Nothing is locked in.
  • No commission, ever. We charge a subscription and take no part of any sale. We don’t buy, sell, or broker equipment.

What happens next

  1. A 20-minute call to understand how you manage idle assets today.
  2. If it’s a fit, we agree the scope and terms of a 90-day pilot in writing.
  3. We load your asset list, and you start using it with your team.

The pilot is free, and it runs alongside whatever you use today — there is nothing to rip out and nothing to migrate.
