How to use this number

This is an estimate, not an accounting figure. Its value is in making the cost visible: idle assets rarely show up as one line, so the total is usually larger than any single budget owner expects.

To sharpen it, replace the percentage with actual figures from your finance team, and run it separately for your oldest assets, which have usually been costing you the longest.

The bigger prize is often not the holding cost at all. If another site is about to buy something you already have sitting idle, redeploying it avoids the purchase entirely.
