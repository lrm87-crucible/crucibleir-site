Where idle assets get stuck

Most companies don’t lack buyers. They lack a single, current picture of what’s sitting idle and what has to happen before it can move.

Nobody sees the whole list

Each plant tracks its own idle equipment in its own spreadsheet. A pump sitting in one plant can’t be found by the plant about to buy a new one.

Paperwork stalls the sale

Decontamination, permits, title checks, and approvals happen over email, so assets sit for months before anyone can list them.

The cost stays invisible

Insurance, taxes, and upkeep keep accruing on assets that produce nothing, and they rarely show up together as one number.

From idle to recovered in four steps

A workflow built around how investment recovery teams actually work, not a generic asset register.

01

Track

Bring in your asset register from a spreadsheet. Every asset gets a record with specs, location, book value, photos, and an owner for the decision.

Common questions

Straight answers about what Crucible is and isn’t.

What is investment recovery?

Investment recovery is the function inside an industrial company that finds the best use or sale for surplus, idle, or obsolete assets, instead of letting them sit or scrapping them by default. Some companies call it surplus asset management or asset recovery.

Do you buy, sell, or auction equipment?

No. Crucible is software only. We don’t run auctions, take commissions, or buy or resell equipment, so your choice of buyer is never influenced by us.

Can we sell through our own buyers and brokers?

Yes. Crucible tracks the sale process, but it doesn’t require you to sell through any particular channel. You can log inquiries and offers from any buyer.

How do we get our existing asset list in?

Start with a CSV or Excel file. During a pilot we help you map your columns and load the list. Integrations with ERP systems such as SAP are planned, and pilot partners help decide which come first.

How is our data protected?

Each client’s data is kept in a separate workspace, and access is controlled by role. Your asset data is used only to run your account. It is never shared with buyers or used for any other business. Details on hosting and security are available to pilot partners on request.

What does it cost?

Pricing will be a simple annual subscription per company. Pilot terms are discussed on a short call, and we’ll be upfront about what a pilot includes before you commit to anything.
