What this site collects

This website doesn’t use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. When you use the request form, your browser opens an email addressed to us containing the details you entered, such as your name, work email, company, role, and notes. We receive that information only if you send the email.

How we use it

We use the details you send to reply to your request and to set up a call. We don’t sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

The calculator

The holding cost calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to us or stored.

The interactive demo

The demo uses sample data and doesn’t save anything you do. Nothing you enter in it leaves your browser.
